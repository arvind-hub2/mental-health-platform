Hi Soldier 👋

Welcome to your specialized command interface. Soldier Mode provides dedicated tools for wellbeing, duty pressure, and high-intensity recovery tailored for uniformed personnel.
Personnel Profile

What: Your identity, rank, and current duty status.
How: View the top-left card in your dashboard.
When: Verify your active status or update your duty location.
Duty Readiness

What: A high-level signal of your operational readiness.
How: Check the "Duty Readiness" pill in your profile.
When: Before starting a shift to ensure you are fit for duty.
Biometric Indicators

What: Estimates of Stress, Fatigue, Sleep, and Resilience.
How: View the Bio-Grid on your dashboard.
When: Monitor trends to avoid burnout during high-tempo operations.
Duty Wellbeing Check-in

What: A specialized form for operational and workload pressure.
How: Fill out the "Duty Pressure Check" at the bottom of the dashboard.
When: After a critical incident or long-duration shift.
Longitudinal Trends

What: A 14-day view of your stress and recovery patterns.
How: Scroll down to the "Longitudinal Wellbeing Trends" chart.
When: To identify cyclical stress peaks over a two-week window.
Unit Risk Level

What: Anonymized aggregated risk state for your entire unit.
How: Found in the "Command Overview" section.
When: To understand if current stress is systemic or individual.
Secure Banner

What: Status indicator for record confidentiality and channel security.
How: Visible at the top of the hero section in Soldier Mode.
When: To ensure you are operating in a secure, authenticated environment.
Welfare Schemes

What: Direct access to personnel benefits and housing support.
How: Use the "Welfare Schemes" card in the welfare grid.
When: To apply for allowances or check support packages.
Confidential Counselling

What: Secure booking for military-specialist psychologists.
How: Click "Book Session" in the Welfare grid or visit the Counsellors page.
When: For trauma support or long-term mental health management.
Emergency Support

What: High-priority escalation for acute stress or crisis.
How: Use the "Emergency Support" card in the welfare grid.
When: Immediate need for urgent human intervention.
Important Information

What: Official directives on wellness protocols and health leave.
How: View the "Important Information" card in the welfare grid.
When: To understand official rights and medical procedures.
Recovery Studio

What: Decompression exercises like Box Breathing and Grounding.
How: Navigate to the "Recovery" section from the navbar.
When: To rapidly downregulate after a high-intensity event.
Human Escalation

What: A direct bridge from AI chat to human support professionals.
How: Click the "Talk to Human" button in the AI chat interface.
When: When AI support is insufficient for the severity of the situation.

12 · PRIVACY & CONSENT

Your wellbeing is personal.

Data collected

Check-ins, screening responses, recovery sessions, AI conversations, and — only with explicit consent — voice and counselling data.

Why we collect it

To surface early signals, recommend helpful practices, and connect you with qualified human care when appropriate.

AI usage

AI is bounded, transparent about limits, and routes serious concerns to a human counsellor or emergency services.

Voice data

Voice is opt-in. Voiceprints are stored encrypted and can be deleted at any time from this page.

Counsellor data

Only the counsellor you book with sees your context — and only what you choose to share.

Retention

You can export or delete your data at any time. Deleting your account anonymises your record.
